What this first pilot teaches

The visual model is intentionally small enough to inspect and replay.

↔

Companies run on handoffs

Sales, engineering, planning, procurement, production, quality, shipping and finance each own a step. A queue or missing dependency crosses team boundaries.

⌁

Capacity makes promises real

The single assembly cell and constrained test bench limit throughput. A promise date depends on both material availability and finite capacity—not just order entry.

✓

Controls matter under pressure

Resequence, expedite or offer a partial shipment. The model shows trade-offs while preserving the quality-release gate and blocking unapproved substitutions.

Research basis & model boundary
Fictional, configurable teaching example; not a real-company clone, operational advice, or digital twin of a real plant. Process shape is grounded in public references. Every order, quantity, time, cost, queue, forecast, capacity and event shown in the simulator is invented. Public context is separately labeled and does not drive the event rules.
